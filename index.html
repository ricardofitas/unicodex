    Supported notation & Unicode limits

    What you can convert

    Greek letters, mathematical symbols and arrows, fractions, roots, superscripts and subscripts, vectors and accents, mathematical alphabets, sums, products, integrals, matrices, cases and aligned equations.

    Paragraphs, accents and line breaks are retained. Markdown code and unknown commands are kept visible.

    What plain Unicode can represent

    Unicode is text. Complex fractions use parentheses and a slash; matrices use multiple lines. Indices without a Unicode glyph keep an explicit _(…) or ^(…) form. The notes flag these adaptations.

    Custom macros, packages and arbitrary TeX programs are not executed. If a dollar sign means currency, keep it escaped as \$ or use Text mode.
